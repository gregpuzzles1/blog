Today is day 17 of the Codevember challenge. Codevember is a coding challenge where people build small projects based on a word or theme for each day in November. I am following [@thespite](https://x.com/thespite) (Jaume Sanchez Elias) on X. He generated an unofficial prompt list for every day. [Jaume’s post](https://x.com/thespite/status/2116974998212772095?a=Ja7aa-sznyd9T6J_JNo&s=46) Today’s day 17 prompt is:

{{< keyword >}}kelp{{< /keyword >}}

This is the prompt I started with and gave to Copilot in VS Code:

{{< alert icon="code" >}}
Theme for the day: kelp

Create Kelp Courier, where you steer a tiny fish through gaps in swaying kelp.
Use three.js and make the site interactive and responsive.
{{< /alert >}}

Today, I decided to try Claude Sonnet 5.5 - High. It gave much better results than GPT Luna 6 - Medium, but it also used more of my Copilot AI credits than normal. Three.js was used, and the page is interactive and responsive.
