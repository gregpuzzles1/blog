Today is day 19 of the Codevember challenge. Codevember is a coding challenge where people build small projects based on a word or theme for each day in November. I am following [@thespite](https://x.com/thespite) (Jaume Sanchez Elias) on X. He generated an unofficial prompt list for every day. [Jaume’s post](https://x.com/thespite/status/2116974998212772095?a=Ja7aa-sznyd9T6J_JNo&s=46) Today’s day 19 prompt is:

{{< keyword >}}erosion{{< /keyword >}}

This is the prompt I started with and gave to the Copilot in VS Code:

{{< alert icon="code" >}}
Theme for the day:

{{< keyword >}}erosion{{< /keyword >}}

The Codevember word for today (the 19th) is: erosion. I want to carve a canyon with a rain painter. User will click or tap the clouds to make rainfall/stop rainfall. Landscape should be similar to what is found in Utah and/or the Grand Canyon. Landscape should have joshua trees, giant yucca, Utah yucca, desert willow, and gambel oak (at higher elevations). Timelapse decades and centuries and should demonstrate the effects of erosion. Viewpoint should be from a drone in the sky - camera should be orbiting. Should be able to rotate 360 degrees, right, left, up, down. Use three.js, webgl, and be real 3D. Site should be interactive and responsive.
{{< /alert >}}

I used Claude Sonnet 5.5 - High again. It is giving much better results than GPT Luna 6 - Medium. It also used more of my Copilot AI credits. Three.js was used. The page is interactive and responsive. I gave it the prompt in VS Code and it churned for about an hour or so. Visually it looks great. Realistically you can get the picture of erosion. I am going to start having ChatGPT help me with my next prompts. I want a more minimal UI experience. Anyway, I had fun with this Day. Keep exploring!
