Today is day 18 of the Codevember challenge. Codevember is a coding challenge where people build small projects based on a word or theme for each day in November. I am following [@thespite](https://x.com/thespite) (Jaume Sanchez Elias) on X. He generated an unofficial prompt list for every day. [Jaume’s post](https://x.com/thespite/status/2116974998212772095?a=Ja7aa-sznyd9T6J_JNo&s=46) Today’s day 18 prompt is:

{{< keyword >}}pendulum{{< /keyword >}}

This is the prompt I started with and gave to Copilot in VS Code:

{{< alert icon="code" >}}
Theme for the day: pendulum

Today the word for Codevember - Day 18 is "Pendulum". Can you create a pendulum or, more precisely, a Newton's Cradle Pendulum?
The cradle should be visible from any angle - 360 degrees - up, down, right, and left.
The page/app should be interactive, responsive, and created with Three.js.
{{< /alert >}}

Today, I used Claude Sonnet 5.5 - High again. It gave much better results than GPT Luna 6 - Medium. It also used more of my Copilot AI credits than normal. Three.js was used. The page is interactive and responsive, and it created a nice Newton's Cradle Pendulum.

Check out the build here: [Codevember Day 18 ~ pendulum](https://gregpuzzles1.github.io/codevember-2026/18/)
